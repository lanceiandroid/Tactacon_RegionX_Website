Plan Your Trip

This is a sample 3-day plan, not a fixed tour. Northern Mindanao is a large region, so give yourself extra travel time and check ferry schedules, weather, and attraction access before leaving.

One possible route

A simple 3-day itinerary

Adjust the plan to your time and transport.

DayPlanNotes
01
Cagayan de Oro		Arrive, explore the city, try local food, and book a guided rafting trip if time allows.Keep the first day flexible in case of travel delays. Use a reputable rafting operator.
02
Bukidnon		Take a day trip to the highlands for views, cooler weather, and an outdoor attraction near Manolo Fortich.Leave early and confirm transport. Bring a light jacket for cooler areas.
03
Camiguin		Start early for the transfer to Balingoan and the ferry to Camiguin. Choose one or two stops such as White Island or the Sunken Cemetery.This can be a packed day. Check current ferry schedules and consider staying overnight if the timing is tight.

Note: The itinerary covers several areas and can involve long transfers. A slower trip focused on one province may be more comfortable.

Before you leave

Travel tips

  • Check weather conditions and transport schedules before leaving.
  • Bring cash for small shops, tricycles, and local transport.
  • Use reef-safe habits, avoid littering, and follow rules in protected areas.
  • For outdoor activities, listen to guides and use the required safety gear.
  • Allow extra time between provinces rather than planning every hour tightly.
