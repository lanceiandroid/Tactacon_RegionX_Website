Places worth the trip

Popular Destinations

Here are five places that show different sides of Northern Mindanao, from natural scenery to outdoor adventures. Check local rules and opening details before visiting.

01 / Island

White Island

This bright sandbar sits off the coast of Camiguin and is known for its clear water and open views of the island's volcanic peaks. Visitors usually reach it by a short boat ride, and there is little shade on the sand.

Location: Mambajao, Camiguin

02 / Historic landmark

Sunken Cemetery

A large cross marks the site where part of an old cemetery sank after volcanic activity in the 1870s. It is a well-known landmark and a quiet place to learn about Camiguin's volcanic history.

Location: Catarman, Camiguin

03 / Waterfall

Maria Cristina Falls

Often called one of Iligan's best-known natural landmarks, the falls drop into the Agus River and are surrounded by thick greenery. The water is also important to the area's hydroelectric power supply, so access and viewing conditions may vary.

Location: Iligan City

04 / Beach

Mantigue Island

Mantigue is a small island with a white beach, shallow clear water, and a green interior. People visit for swimming, snorkeling, and spending a slower day close to the sea.

Location: Mahinog, Camiguin

05 / Outdoor adventure

Cagayan de Oro River

The river is popular for guided white-water rafting, with routes that vary in difficulty and water conditions. First-time visitors should book with a licensed or established operator and follow the guide's safety instructions.

Location: Cagayan de Oro area, Misamis Oriental
