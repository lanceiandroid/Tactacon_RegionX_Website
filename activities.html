Things to do

Use these ideas to start planning your visit.

01 / Outdoor

Adventure

  • Try guided white-water rafting on the Cagayan de Oro River.
  • Go island-hopping or snorkeling around Camiguin.
  • Explore outdoor parks and zipline attractions in the Bukidnon highlands.

02 / Outdoors

Nature & Relaxation

  • Visit waterfalls and scenic viewpoints.
  • Spend time on the beaches of White Island or Mantigue Island.
  • Look for hot or cold springs in Camiguin and plan time to rest between stops.

03 / Community

Culture & Festivals

  • Learn about the Kaamulan Festival and the Indigenous communities of Bukidnon.
  • Visit during Camiguin's Lanzones Festival if the dates fit your schedule.
  • Explore local museums, churches, markets, and community events respectfully.

04 / Local food

Food to Try

  • Try Camiguin pastel, a soft bun often filled with sweet custard.
  • Look for binaki, a steamed corn cake wrapped in corn husks.
  • Sample local seafood, grilled dishes, and regional snacks from local sellers.

Small local eateries and markets are a good way to discover everyday regional food.
