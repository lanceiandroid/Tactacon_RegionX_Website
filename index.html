A region with many sides

Many places, one region.

Northern Mindanao is in the north-central part of Mindanao. Its five provinces are Bukidnon, Camiguin, Lanao del Norte, Misamis Occidental, and Misamis Oriental. Cagayan de Oro City is the regional hub, and the area is known for its coastlines, highlands, waterfalls, outdoor activities, and mix of cultures.

Whether you enjoy a quiet beach, a day outdoors, or trying food from a local market, there is something different to see in every part of the region.
